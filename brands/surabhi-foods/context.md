## Brand-specific carry-outs

- Preserve the official site's unresolved 1989/2006 history contradiction; do not choose one without confirmation.
- Voice is warm, culinary, sensory, benefit-led, and written for Indian home cooks.
- Use British/Indian spelling where the site does: “flavour,” “colours.”
- Keep claims product-specific and sourced; do not invent health, nutrition, sourcing, or certification details.
- Shopify is confirmed. GSC and analytics are not yet connected, so never invent performance data.
- Existing blogs and recipes are the primary writing-style references.
