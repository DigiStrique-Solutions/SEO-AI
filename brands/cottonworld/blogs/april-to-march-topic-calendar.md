## Existing-topic exclusions

Do not commission generic versions of these themes unless they replace the current broken pages:

- Why natural cotton/fabric matters
- Slow living and dressing with purpose
- How to build a timeless wardrobe or choose clothes that last
- Generic comfort-meets-style/everyday-fashion content

## Recommended rollout

1. Publish April-June first: summer comparisons, fabric literacy, workwear, breathable-fabric guidance, travel, monsoon dressing and monsoon care.
2. Build internal links from each post to the relevant Cotton, Linen, Bamboo, Work, Travel or Summer collection.
3. Add answer-first sections and tightly scoped FAQs, but only use FAQ structured data when the questions and answers are visible on the page.
4. Validate demand and wording in GSC before locking briefs. Until then, treat the target keywords as hypotheses rather than volume-backed priorities.
5. Align movable festivals to the actual publishing year and publish occasion posts 4-6 weeks before the event.
