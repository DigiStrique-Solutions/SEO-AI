## Item 1

Item ID: `local-seo.17-local-ai-and-answer-engine-readiness.472aa2af`

Original checklist item: Business entity details are clear and consistent across website, Google, Bing, Apple, major directories, and review platforms.

### What It Means

Confirm whether the audited scope satisfies this requirement: "Business entity details are clear and consistent across website, Google, Bing, Apple, major directories, and review platforms." In practice, this means the reviewer needs concrete evidence, not a general impression.

### Why It Affects SEO

Useful, source-backed content improves relevance, user confidence, and eligibility for sensitive or competitive queries. Weak or unsupported content can suppress trust and conversions. Local SEO depends on accurate entity, location, category, review, and profile data. Inconsistency can weaken relevance, prominence, and customer trust.

### How To Verify

1. Identify the exact URL, template, brand workspace, connector account, or page group in scope.
2. Collect evidence from: Manual/local SERP, Firecrawl, Playwright, GBP, Yelp/Tripadvisor/verticals, GSC, Human/context, Google Maps, GA4, GSC Links, Bing Webmaster Tools, manual SERP.
3. Record pass, fail, not_applicable, or not_checked_blocked in the audit matrix with an artifact reference.

### Evidence Sources

Manual/local SERP, Firecrawl, Playwright, GBP, Yelp/Tripadvisor/verticals, GSC, Human/context, Google Maps, GA4, GSC Links, Bing Webmaster Tools, manual SERP

### Pass Criteria
