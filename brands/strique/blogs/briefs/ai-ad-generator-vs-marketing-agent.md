# Content Brief

## Recommended Topic

Compare an AI ad generator with an AI marketing agent. The point of view: creative output matters, but customer acquisition cost is shaped by the full operating loop around creative, audience, offer, landing page, launch, reporting, and follow-up.

## Why This Topic

The Strique keyword file maps `ai ad generator`, `ai advertisement generator`, `ai generated advertising`, `ai ad maker`, and `ai ad creator` to commercial product intent. Existing Strique blog coverage already includes AI ad analytics, AI ad optimization, CAC reduction, and AI marketing agent education. This post should connect those adjacent themes without becoming another tool list.

## Search Intent

Searchers likely want a way to make ads faster. The article should meet that intent, then explain where generators stop and where a marketing agent loop becomes useful for teams responsible for CAC, ROAS, creative fatigue, and channel coordination.

## Content Structure

1. Open with the direct distinction between ad generation and marketing execution.
2. Define what an AI ad generator can reasonably do.
3. Show why creative alone does not control CAC.
4. Explain the agent loop around ads: context, brief, create, launch, measure, learn.
5. List the handoffs a generator leaves behind.
6. Give evaluation criteria for growth teams.
7. Close with Strique's fit as the operating loop around paid media.
