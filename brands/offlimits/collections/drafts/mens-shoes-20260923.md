## Check the details that affect your fit

The product page should be part of the decision, not the last stop. Look at the closure, upper material, sole and insock. Those details give you a clearer sense of the structure, adjustability and underfoot feel a pair may offer.

Lace-ups let you fine-tune the fit. Consider the socks you normally wear, leave enough room at the toes and check the live size selector before adding a pair to your bag. Colour and size availability can change, so the product page is the right place to confirm what is available now.

If you are deciding between a running shoe and a training shoe, the [running versus gym shoes guide](https://offlimits.co.in/blogs/blogs/running-vs-gym-shoes-uncover-the-difference-between-running-vs-gym-shoes) helps explain the different movement priorities behind each category. Use it to narrow your search before comparing specific models.
