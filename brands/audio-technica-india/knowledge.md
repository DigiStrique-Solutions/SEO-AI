## Positioning

Audio-Technica presents 60+ years of Japanese innovation and quality, with a sustained focus on natural, accurate audio. The India site covers consumer listening and professional audio: headphones, microphones, turntables, cartridges and commercial-audio solutions.

## Audience and conversion

Primary audiences include music listeners, audiophiles, creators, podcasters, musicians, performers, gamers and commercial AV buyers. The primary site actions are ecommerce purchase (`Add to cart`) and product enquiry.

## Messaging guidance

Use a confident, precise, product-led voice. Anchor copy in product category, use case and substantiated technical capability. Preserve the brand's recurring themes: precision engineering, natural and accurate sound, Japanese heritage, analogue listening, and audio for creators and enthusiasts.

Avoid unsupported superlatives, unverified India-specific service claims, and claims of category leadership without a source.

## Key site areas

- `/` — catalog, product CTAs and category-level positioning.
- `/about/` — history, founding, analogue heritage and technology narrative.
- `/category/` and `/shop/` — category and product discovery.
- `/blog/` — editorial inventory.
- `/contact/` — contact route.
