## Item 1

Item ID: `site-architecture-seo.18-local-architecture.9b0a49db`

Original checklist item: Location pages map to real locations or service areas.

### What It Means

Confirm whether the audited scope satisfies this requirement: "Location pages map to real locations or service areas." In practice, this means the reviewer needs concrete evidence, not a general impression.

### Why It Affects SEO

Architecture controls how users and crawlers find important pages. Good internal linking helps distribute context, priority, and crawl paths.

### How To Verify

1. Identify the exact URL, template, brand workspace, connector account, or page group in scope.
