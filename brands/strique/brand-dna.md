### Business Description

Strique is an agentic AI marketing platform and AI marketing OS for growth teams. It runs marketing work through one agent loop: plan the work, ship it, measure outcomes, and feed learnings back into the next run.

The product covers paid media, SEO, content, lifecycle, reporting, research, generative content, scheduled automations, approval gates, closed-loop measurement, reusable skills, persistent memory, and interactive Canvas reports. The public site positions Strique as a replacement for fragmented dashboards, agencies, point tools, and manual coordination.

## Brand Aesthetic

Minimal, spacious, product-led SaaS aesthetic with a strong operator feel. The site uses large centered headlines, restrained grayscale surfaces, rounded pill navigation, black CTAs, and small proof points rather than decorative illustration.

Visual hierarchy is quiet and confident: lots of white space, sharp typography, evidence blocks, customer logos, case-study cards, integration icons, pricing tables, and product/report language. The brand feels modern, technical, and growth-focused without looking like an enterprise dashboard.

## Brand Values

- Opinionated execution: Strique favors making a call, shipping a recommendation, and refining from feedback.
- Measurable outcomes: customer proof and product language emphasize ROAS, CAC, revenue, conversion, spend, and compounding learning.
- Closed-loop learning: the product is built around reading back results and improving future work.
- Human control for high-stakes actions: ad launches, bulk email, and public posts require explicit approval by default.
- Team fit over tool sprawl: Strique says it meets teams in existing channels such as Slack, WhatsApp, email, and web.
- Traceability and trust: security pages emphasize versioning, checkpoints, audit logs, org isolation, encryption, and approval gates.
- Practical autonomy: the brand pushes autonomy, but frames it as supervised execution rather than reckless automation.

## Tone of Voice

Direct, confident, operator-centric, and plain-spoken. Strique uses short declarative claims, concrete numbers, and pragmatic comparisons. The voice is comfortable being opinionated, but it explains the tradeoff instead of sounding fluffy.

Common voice patterns:

- Outcome-first headlines.
- Short sentences with a strategic edge.
- Metrics over adjectives.
- Crisp contrasts between agents, dashboards, agencies, and point tools.
- Occasional conversational lines such as "That was a mouthful" or "No slide deck."

Avoid generic AI hype. Prefer specific workflows, measurable results, and clear business stakes.

## Target Audience

Primary audience:

- Ecommerce and DTC brands that need paid media, creative, lifecycle, content, SEO, reporting, and optimization in one loop.
- Growth teams that want more output without adding agencies, vendors, or headcount linearly.

Role-based audience:

- D2C founders.
- Marketing managers.
- CMOs and VPs of Marketing.
- Paid media specialists.
- Freelancers and agencies.
