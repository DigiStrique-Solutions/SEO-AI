# Reference: Closing the Loop in AI Marketing

**Summary:** Argues that generating content quickly is trivial; the real advantage is systems that read back what happened, learn, and compound. Positions Strique as a true AI marketing agent by implementing all four closed-loop components.

**Key points:**
- Most AI marketing tools stop at content generation — no measurement, no learning.
- True closed-loop systems observe outcomes and adjust, running daily vs. weekly human cycles.
- Four required components: analytics ingestion across channels, attribution modeling, persistent memory, explicit decision policies.
- Proper attribution avoids over-favoring bottom-funnel channels at the expense of awareness.
- Organizational memory across campaigns compounds advantage.
