## Questions about the OFFLIMITS men’s collection

### What are athleisure shoes for men?

Athleisure shoes bring athletic styling into casual outfits. The term describes a broad category, so choose by the individual shoe’s intended use, fit and construction. A sporty appearance alone does not tell you which workout a shoe suits.

### Can I use the same shoes for the gym and everyday wear?

Some running and gym styles can also work with casual clothing. Start with the demands of your workout, then consider everyday styling. For strength-focused sessions, pay attention to stability as well as cushioning; our running-versus-gym guide explains the distinction.
