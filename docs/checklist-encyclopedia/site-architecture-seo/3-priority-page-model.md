## Item 1

Item ID: `site-architecture-seo.3-priority-page-model.38711aa4`

Original checklist item: Priority pages are ranked by business value, not only traffic.

### What It Means

Confirm whether the audited scope satisfies this requirement: "Priority pages are ranked by business value, not only traffic." In practice, this means the reviewer needs concrete evidence, not a general impression.

### Why It Affects SEO

Architecture controls how users and crawlers find important pages. Good internal linking helps distribute context, priority, and crawl paths. SEO decisions need reliable measurement. This check prevents prioritization based on incomplete, unscoped, or misleading data.
