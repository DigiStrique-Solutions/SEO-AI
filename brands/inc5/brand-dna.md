### Business Description

Inc5 is an Indian fashion footwear and bags brand with an ecommerce storefront and offline retail presence. The public storefront positions Inc5 as an exclusive fashion footwear brand and sells women's footwear, men's footwear, handbags, wallets, clearance products, bestsellers, and new arrivals.

The brand history page describes Inc5 as established in 1998, starting from a 100 sq. ft. store at Mumbai's Heera Panna. Inc5 says it is now present across 50+ cities, 20+ states, and 90+ stores nationwide, with availability through exclusive stores and retail destinations such as Lifestyle, Centro, and Shoppers Stop.

The site combines online shopping, store discovery, order tracking, returns/exchanges, care instructions, customer reviews, blog guides, and loyalty points through Club5.

## Brand Aesthetic

Fashion ecommerce with a practical catalog-first layout: category navigation, sale banners, product collections, review proof, social links, store locator, account/cart flows, and checkout-oriented UX.

The storefront is built around product photography, large collection banners, filters, availability and sorting controls, product cards, review snippets, and utility links for delivery, returns, store locator, FAQs, and care instructions.

## Brand Values

- Comfort.
- Craftsmanship.
- Contemporary design.
- Accessible premium footwear and bags across online and offline channels.
- Product care and longevity, supported by footwear and handbag care instructions.
- Customer support for sizing, delivery, returns, exchanges, and bulk or specific product inquiries.

## Tone of Voice

Retail-focused, direct, category-led, and practical. Inc5 uses promotional ecommerce language, category labels, product-benefit copy, seasonal style guidance, care instructions, and support-led FAQ language.

Keep copy clear, product-specific, and grounded in real product details such as material, fit, heel type, occasion, comfort, size, availability, delivery, returns, exchanges, and customer review language.

## Target Audience

- Women shopping for heels, transparent heels, sandals, mules, flats, ethnic footwear, boots, wedges, platform heels, pumps, comfort footwear, and bags.
- Men shopping for driving shoes, formal shoes, sandals, slip-ons, boots, and large-size footwear.
- Shoppers looking for handbags, sling bags, clutches, shoulder bags, wallets, backpacks, and party or everyday bags.
- Indian ecommerce shoppers who care about offline store access, delivery, returns, exchanges, size, comfort, and product care.
