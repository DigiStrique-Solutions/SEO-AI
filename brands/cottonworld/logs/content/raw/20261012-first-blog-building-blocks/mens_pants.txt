We use premium natural fabrics like 100% breathable cotton, airy linen, and stretch-cotton blends to create trousers that actually move with you. Whether you need crisp chinos for the office or relaxed linen pants for the weekend, we've got your lower half covered.

Why Premium Natural Fabrics Matter
We are entirely obsessed with natural materials. Here is why our fabrics make a massive difference to how you feel throughout the day.
1. 100% Breathable Cotton
Synthetic pants trap sweat. Pure cotton doesn't. It allows air to circulate, keeping you cool and fresh. Plus, it naturally softens over time, meaning your pants actually get more comfortable the more you wear and wash them.
2. The Linen Advantage
Linen is nature's built-in air conditioner. It wicks moisture away from your skin rapidly and dries almost instantly. It has a natural, slightly textured look that adds instant character and sophistication to any outfit.
3. A Touch of Elastane
For our tailored and slim-fit styles, we weave a tiny bit of premium elastane into the cotton. This gives the fabric a subtle, resilient four-way stretch. You can bend, sit, and move freely without feeling like the seams are going to burst.

How to Make Your Cotton & Linen Pants Last
Take care of your natural fabrics, and they will easily last you for years.
1. Wash Cold:
Always wash your pants in cold water to prevent shrinking and fading.
2. Inside Out:
Turn them inside out before tossing them in the machine. It protects the fabric's surface color.
3. Skip the Dryer:
High heat destroys cotton and linen fibers. Air dry your pants in the shade.
4. Ironing:
Iron cotton while it's slightly damp, or use a good steamer. For linen, embrace the natural wrinkles ”it's part of the fabric's charm!

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
1. Are cotton pants suitable for formal office wear?
Yes, absolutely. Our tailored chinos and structured cotton pants offer a clean, professional silhouette. Stick to classic colors like Navy, Black, or Khaki, and pair them with a tucked-in button-down shirt and formal shoes.
2. Do linen pants wrinkle easily?
Linen naturally has a bit of texture and will form soft wrinkles as you move. That's actually the beauty of the fabric! It gives off a relaxed, premium look. If you want a sharper finish for an event, a quick steam does the trick perfectly.
3. What's the difference between chinos and regular cotton pants?
Chinos are typically made from a specific twill weave cotton, giving them a slightly dressier, smoother finish. Regular cotton pants might feature different weaves, often feeling a bit more relaxed and casual in their drape.
4. Will my pants shrink after washing?
Because we use high-quality natural fibers, there might be very minimal shrinkage after the very first wash. Just follow the care labelâ€”wash cold and never use a hot tumble dryer ”and they will hold their shape nicely.
5. What is the best pant for a hot Indian summer?
You cannot beat our pure Linen Pants or our ultra-lightweight Cotton Pants. They are incredibly breathable, promote airflow, and pull moisture away from your body, keeping you cool all day long.
