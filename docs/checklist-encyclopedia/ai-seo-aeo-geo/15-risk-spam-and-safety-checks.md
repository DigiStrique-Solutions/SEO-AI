## Item 1

Item ID: `ai-seo-aeo-geo.15-risk-spam-and-safety-checks.907d59fd`

Original checklist item: No scaled AI-generated content exists without real review, usefulness, and differentiation.

### What It Means

Confirm whether the audited scope satisfies this requirement: "No scaled AI-generated content exists without real review, usefulness, and differentiation." In practice, this means the reviewer needs concrete evidence, not a general impression.

### Why It Affects SEO

Useful, source-backed content improves relevance, user confidence, and eligibility for sensitive or competitive queries. Weak or unsupported content can suppress trust and conversions. Local SEO depends on accurate entity, location, category, review, and profile data. Inconsistency can weaken relevance, prominence, and customer trust.

### How To Verify

1. Identify the exact URL, template, brand workspace, connector account, or page group in scope.
2. Collect evidence from: GSC, Manual AI SERP, Firecrawl, Playwright, CMS/code, Human/context, Off-page, GBP, Google Maps, GA4, GSC Links, Bing Webmaster Tools, manual SERP, saved prompt set, manual AI SERP.
3. Record prompt wording, platform, location, date, account state, cited URLs, and answer summary.
4. Record pass, fail, not_applicable, or not_checked_blocked in the audit matrix with an artifact reference.

### Evidence Sources

GSC, Manual AI SERP, Firecrawl, Playwright, CMS/code, Human/context, Off-page, GBP, Google Maps, GA4, GSC Links, Bing Webmaster Tools, manual SERP, saved prompt set, manual AI SERP

### Pass Criteria
