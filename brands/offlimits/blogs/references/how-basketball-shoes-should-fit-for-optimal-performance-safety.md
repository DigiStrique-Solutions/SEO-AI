# Reference: How Basketball Shoes Should Fit for Optimal Performance & Safety

**Summary:** A fit-focused guide detailing exactly how basketball shoes should sit on the foot to improve performance and prevent injury. It layers in a price-tier breakdown and high-ankle vs low-top guidance.

**Key points:**
- Defines the ideal fit: snug heel lockdown, thumb-width toe room, arch support, firm midfoot, structured flex
- Compares high-ankle (stability) vs low-top (speed) designs
- Presents budget tiers: under 2000, under 3000, under 5000 collections
- Buying tips: test with game socks, move laterally, shop later in day, avoid oversizing
- FAQ covers shoe types, choosing, what makes them special, and lifespan (3-6 months)
