# Reference: The Story Behind Our Brand - Offlimits

**Summary:** The founding-story page explaining that Offlimits was created after observing Indian athletes training barefoot or in poor gear because quality equipment was too costly. It lays out the 'go beyond limits' brand philosophy and logo symbolism.

**Key points:**
- Origin: filling the gap for affordable, performance-driven footwear and apparel in India
- Brand ethos centres on defying self-imposed limits ('Living Off Limits' as a mindset)
- Explains the twin-arrow logo ('The Untold'): gear/technology plus motivation/belief
- Emphasises relentless pursuit of comfort- and performance-enhancing technology
- Foundational brand-DNA reference for voice, mission, and positioning
