## Make the product page do the hard work

A Best Sellers collection is a browse route, not a substitute for checking fit. Open the product page for the pair that catches your eye and look at the closure, upper material, sole and insock. These details help you judge how the shoe may feel and where it may fit into your week.

Lace-ups let you adjust the fit; an upper and sole can affect the kind of structure and underfoot feel you prefer. Consider the socks you normally wear, leave enough toe room and check the live size selector before adding a pair to your bag. Individual colour and size availability can change.
