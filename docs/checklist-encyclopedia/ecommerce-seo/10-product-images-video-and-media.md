## Item 1

Item ID: `ecommerce-seo.10-product-images-video-and-media.f1c872b5`

Original checklist item: Primary product images show the actual product clearly and match the product or variant.

### What It Means

Confirm whether the audited scope satisfies this requirement: "Primary product images show the actual product clearly and match the product or variant." In practice, this means the reviewer needs concrete evidence, not a general impression.

### Why It Affects SEO

Product visibility depends on consistent page, schema, and feed data. Gaps can reduce merchant listing eligibility, product rich results, and purchase confidence.

### How To Verify

1. Identify the exact URL, template, brand workspace, connector account, or page group in scope.
2. Collect evidence from: Firecrawl, Playwright, GMC, Shopify, CMS/code, CrUX/LH, OSS, Shopify or CMS.

4. Compare visible page content, structured data, and feed or platform values for consistency.
