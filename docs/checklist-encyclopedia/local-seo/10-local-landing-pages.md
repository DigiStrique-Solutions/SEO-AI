## Item 1

Item ID: `local-seo.10-local-landing-pages.c1813f1d`

Original checklist item: Each location or service-area page has a clear local purpose and target audience.

### What It Means

Confirm whether the audited scope satisfies this requirement: "Each location or service-area page has a clear local purpose and target audience." In practice, this means the reviewer needs concrete evidence, not a general impression.

### Why It Affects SEO

Intent alignment affects whether the page deserves to show for the target query. This check keeps the page focused on the searcher's job instead of only matching keywords. Local SEO depends on accurate entity, location, category, review, and profile data. Inconsistency can weaken relevance, prominence, and customer trust.
