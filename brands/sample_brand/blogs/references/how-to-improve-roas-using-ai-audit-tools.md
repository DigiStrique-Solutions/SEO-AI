# Reference: How to improve ROAS using AI ad audit tools like Strique

**Summary:** Explains how AI-powered ad audits accelerate ROAS recovery by finding wasteful spend and tired creatives faster than manual review. Argues decision speed now matters more than reporting, with unified platforms surfacing actionable recommendations across scattered ad data.

**Key points:**
- ROAS decays when audits are infrequent; audience overlap and poor SKU prioritization hide beneath surface metrics.
- AI audits give "speed with context" — flag expensive low-conversion campaigns and fatigued creatives fast.
- Unified data (Meta, Google, Shopify, GA4) in one workspace removes fragmentation.
- Metrics to watch: spend efficiency vs. net sales, creative fatigue velocity, SKU-level contribution, funnel leakage, channel overlap.
- Five-step play: full account audit → prioritize budget leaks → scale profitable SKUs → refresh creatives → weekly audits.
