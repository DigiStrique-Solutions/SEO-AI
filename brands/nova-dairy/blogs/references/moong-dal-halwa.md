## Meta description

Rich, slow-cooked moong dal dessert made with ghee, milk, and nuts.

## Summary

This recipe describes a slow-cooked moong dal halwa with a roasted aroma and grainy texture. It uses Nova Dairy Shudh Ghee, pasteurised milk and skimmed milk powder and presents the dish as a festive or winter dessert.

## Key points

- Soaks and coarsely grinds yellow moong dal before roasting.
- Uses Nova Dairy Shudh Ghee for the roasting stage.
- Adds pasteurised milk, skimmed milk powder, sugar, cardamom, saffron and nuts.
- Emphasises patient roasting to develop colour and aroma.
- Includes serving, refrigeration and approximate nutrition guidance.
