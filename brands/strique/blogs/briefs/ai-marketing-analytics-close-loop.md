# Content Brief

## Recommended Topic

Explain AI marketing analytics as a closed-loop practice: define the question before launch, capture the inputs, connect spend and outcomes, turn reporting into decisions, and preserve learning for the next campaign.

## Why This Topic

The Strique keyword file includes `ai marketing analytics`, `marketing automation reporting`, and `ai ad optimization`. The brand DNA gives the product angle: Strique emphasizes closed-loop learning, PostHog as the product and behavioral analytics source, GSC through Composio, and an agent loop that reads results back into future work.

This article should avoid generic analytics advice. It should show how reporting becomes useful only when it changes the next action.

## Search Intent

Searchers are evaluating analytics and reporting workflows tied to AI-assisted campaigns. They need an operational model, not another dashboard definition.

## Content Structure

1. Open with the reporting failure after campaign launch.
2. Define what closing the loop means.
3. Show the campaign evidence that should be captured before launch.
4. Explain how AI marketing analytics should connect channel, creative, audience, page, and conversion signals.
5. Give the post-campaign review sequence.
6. Show how Strique's loop fits without inventing performance claims.
