# Content Brief

## Recommended Topic

SEO automation as a closed-loop operating system: audit, prioritize, brief, ship, measure, and learn.

## Why This Topic

The keyword list has a clear commercial SEO automation cluster:

| Cluster | Directional monthly volume | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| SEO automation | 13,320 | Includes `seo automation`, `automated seo optimization`, `automatic seo optimization`, and `seo automation platform`. |

| AI ad generation | 11,460 | Existing blog coverage already leans into ad analytics and ad optimization. |
| AI marketing agent | 1,670 | Strong product fit and existing blog support. |

The audit evidence points to a practical angle: SEO automation should find and fix repeatable site issues, not only generate articles. The repeated audit findings include image alt text gaps, multi-H1 issues, sitemap and canonical hygiene, accessible control gaps, and missing analytics evidence.

## Search Intent

Searchers looking for `seo automation` are likely evaluating tools or workflows. The article should help them understand what to automate, what not to automate, and how a marketing agent loop is different from a content generator.

## Content Structure

1. Define SEO automation in one direct answer.
2. Explain why most SEO automation fails.
3. Show the six-step agent loop.
4. List the jobs worth automating.
5. Show what should stay human-reviewed.
6. Give a simple implementation path.
7. Close with a Strique CTA.
