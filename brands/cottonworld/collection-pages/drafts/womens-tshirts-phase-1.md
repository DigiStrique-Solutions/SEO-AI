## A practical styling guide

T-shirts can be part of different outfits without needing complicated styling. Begin with the plan for the day and the clothes you already feel comfortable in.

- **For everyday errands:** Pair a regular-fit T-shirt with [women's pants](https://cottonworld.net/collections/womens-pants). Choose a colour that works with more than one bottom if you want a straightforward repeat outfit.
- **For a workday:** If your setting allows a casual layer, wear a simple tee beneath an open shirt or a light outer layer. Check the neckline and sleeve so the layers sit the way you prefer.
- **For a relaxed plan:** A coloured or printed tee can be the main detail in an otherwise familiar outfit. Keep the rest simple if that feels more like you.
- **For travel:** Choose a tee that can pair with several pieces already in your bag. Fit and fabric preference matter more than packing a different option for every day.
- **For layering:** Compare the tee's fit and sleeve with the shirt, cardigan or jacket you plan to wear over it. A layer should feel comfortable when you move.
- **For repeat wear:** Repeating a favourite is a practical choice. Change the layer or bottom when you want variety, and keep the T-shirt that already feels right in rotation.

The collection is a place to compare options, not a checklist to complete. A few considered choices can make everyday dressing feel less like a separate task.
