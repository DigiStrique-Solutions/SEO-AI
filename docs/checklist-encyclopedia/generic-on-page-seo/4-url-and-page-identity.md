## Item 1

Item ID: `generic-on-page-seo.4-url-and-page-identity.f3246780`

Original checklist item: The URL is readable, stable, lowercase, and uses hyphens between words.

### What It Means

Confirm whether the audited scope satisfies this requirement: "The URL is readable, stable, lowercase, and uses hyphens between words." In practice, this means the reviewer needs concrete evidence, not a general impression.

### Why It Affects SEO

Intent alignment affects whether the page deserves to show for the target query. This check keeps the page focused on the searcher's job instead of only matching keywords.
