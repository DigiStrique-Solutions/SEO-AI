Paneer Butter Masala is a rich and creamy Indian curry made with soft paneer in a flavourful tomato-based gravy.

## Summary

This recipe explains a North Indian paneer curry built on a tomato, onion and cashew gravy. It uses Nova Dairy Paneer, Butter, Cow Ghee and Dairy Creamer and is positioned for dinners, festive meals and family gatherings.

## Key points

- Uses paneer cubes in a tomato, onion and cashew gravy.
- Features Nova Butter and Nova Dairy Cow Ghee during cooking.
- Finishes the gravy with Nova Dairy Creamer, garam masala and kasuri methi.
