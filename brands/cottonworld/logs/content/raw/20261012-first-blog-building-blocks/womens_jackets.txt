Choose a collar, closure and pockets
Small construction details can change the way a jacket works with an outfit. Think about whether you prefer a collar that frames the neck or a simpler neckline that sits over your top. Compare buttoned and zipped openings where available, and look at the jacket worn both open and closed. Choose the details that feel useful for the places you go and the layers you already own.
Pockets are another practical detail to compare. Look at their position and opening in the photographs, and check the description for the jacket you like. Consider whether you need a place for small essentials or usually carry a bag. Pocket placement can also change the outline of a jacket, so view it alongside the collar, hem and overall shape.
Sleeve length can help you choose a useful layer. Consider how much arm coverage you want and whether you expect to wear a top underneath. Look at the cuff and sleeve shape, especially if you like to push your sleeves up. Compare these details with the way you move through your day, from time at a desk to walking outside.
A simple styling guide
Begin with what the day asks of you. A jacket can be an extra layer for a commute, an afternoon out or a plan that carries into the evening. Pair it with clothes that already feel familiar, and let the details do only as much as you want them to.
For an everyday outing:
Wear a jacket over a simple top with women’s pants. Choose a fit that feels comfortable as you move through errands or a walk.
For a workday:
A solid colour can sit easily alongside pieces you already wear to work. Check the collar and opening to decide whether you prefer it buttoned or open.
For a relaxed plan:
A boxy fit gives a different outline from a regular fit. Keep the rest of the combination simple if you want the jacket to be the main layer.
For travel:
Consider how the jacket works with more than one outfit in your bag. A familiar colour can make it easier to repeat the layer across different plans.
For layering:
Check sleeve and shoulder details against the piece you plan to wear underneath. Comfort when moving matters more than a label alone.
For repeat wear:
Bring the same jacket into different combinations by changing the top, bottom or footwear. Repeating a useful piece can keep everyday choices straightforward.
These are ideas to adapt, not rules to follow. The aim is to choose a layer that makes sense for the day and leaves your attention for everything else happening in it.

Frequently asked questions
How should a women's jacket fit over a top?
Allow enough room across the shoulders, bust and arms for the layer underneath. You should be able to reach forward and fasten the jacket without pulling. Compare its measurements with a jacket you already wear, using a similar base layer.
What is the difference between a regular and a boxy jacket?
A boxy jacket has a wider, straighter outline through the body. A regular fit gives a more conventional shape, though the exact cut varies. Look at the length as well as the width, and choose the silhouette that works with your preferred tops and bottoms.
Can I wear a jacket over a dress?
Yes. Compare the jacket's hem with the waist and shape of the dress. A shorter jacket can leave more of the dress visible, while a longer layer creates another outline. Make sure the sleeves and shoulders feel comfortable together.
What should I wear under a casual jacket?
A plain T-shirt makes an easy base, while a blouse can give the outfit a more dressed feel. Choose a neckline that sits comfortably with the jacket's collar. Keep the sleeves smooth underneath so the layers allow you to move freely.
How can I style a boxy jacket with pants?
Start with pants you already like and compare the proportions in a mirror. A straighter or closer leg can balance the jacket's width. If you prefer roomy pants too, a tucked-in or shorter base top can help keep the waist visible.
What makes a jacket useful for travel?
It should work with several of your packed outfits and leave room for the layers you need. Consider how easily you can carry it when removed, along with its pockets and fastening. Check care needs before packing, particularly for a longer trip.
How should I clean a cotton or linen-blend jacket?
Use the washing or cleaning method specified on its label. Check the instructions for drying and ironing too, as a jacket's construction can affect care. Do not assume that the directions for a cotton shirt also apply to a jacket.
