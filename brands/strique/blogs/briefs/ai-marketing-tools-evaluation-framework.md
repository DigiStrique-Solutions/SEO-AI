# Content Brief

## Recommended Topic

An evaluation framework for AI marketing tools, written for buyers who search with best and top language but need criteria rather than an unsupported ranked list.

## Why This Topic

The keyword file includes `best ai marketing tools`, `best ai tools for marketing`, `top ai marketing platforms`, and `ai marketing tools free`. Because those queries carry ranking language, the article must be explicit: this is a criteria-based framework, not a market ranking, and it does not claim Strique is objectively best.

## Search Intent

Searchers are comparing options, budgets, and categories. They likely need a way to choose between point tools, free utilities, platform suites, and agentic systems.

## Content Structure

1. Explain the real question behind best/top queries.
2. State that the article is not a ranked list.
3. Give evaluation criteria: workflow coverage, context, channel depth, connectors, approvals, reporting, cost, ownership, and learning.
4. Explain where free tools fit.
5. Call out red flags.
6. Position Strique as one agent-loop option without objective superiority claims.
7. Close with a decision worksheet.
