Made for warm days and easy plans, this men's mustard linen cotton shirt brings together the natural character of linen and the familiar comfort of cotton. Its solid woven fabric is composed of 55% linen and 45% cotton, while the regular fit keeps the shape relaxed without looking oversized.

The band collar gives the shirt a clean, modern line. Half sleeves, a pull-on opening with button detailing, and a rounded hem make it an easy choice for casual wear. With no pockets to interrupt the front, the mustard colour and simple silhouette do the work.

Styling note

Wear it untucked with off-white linen trousers for a tonal warm-weather look, or pair it with navy chinos or denim for an easy contrast. Add sandals for a relaxed afternoon; switch to loafers when the plan calls for something sharper.

Care

Machine wash cold. Wash dark colours separately. Do not bleach. Remove promptly and dry in the shade.
