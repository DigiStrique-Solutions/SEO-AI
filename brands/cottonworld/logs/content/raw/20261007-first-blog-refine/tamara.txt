Closet full of clothes and nothing to wear
If you've ever stood in a towel, running late, staring at a full wardrobe with nothing to wear, this is for you...

There is a very specific kind of despair that only happens while fully clothed, or rather, while trying to become fully clothed, standing in front of a wardrobe that is objectively, aggressively full. Or even worse when you are packing and standing desperate over an empty suitcase and a full wardrobe yet really dont know what to pack for the next trip. I know it well. I have stood there. I have owned four hundred things and worn six of them on rotation like a woman under house arrest who was permitted only a single carry-on. And the great cosmic joke of it is that the fullness is the problem. Not the emptiness. We have been sold a story that says the answer to "I have nothing to wear" is "buy more," when in fact more is precisely what put us here, sweating gently in a towel at 8:47 in the morning, late, and furious about it. And trust me, I have been there.
So let me tell you what is actually going on, and then let me tell you what to do, because I am nothing if not a woman who has turned her anxieties into a full functioning machine.
