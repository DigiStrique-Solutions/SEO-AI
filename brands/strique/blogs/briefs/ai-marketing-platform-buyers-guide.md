# Content Brief

## Recommended Topic

A buyer guide for growth teams evaluating an AI marketing platform: what the platform must connect, where dashboards fall short, and which approval, memory, reporting, and execution controls matter.

## Why This Topic

The Strique keyword file maps `ai marketing platform` and `artificial intelligence marketing platform` to the homepage, while `ai marketing software` and `ai marketing os` map to product intent. That makes this a commercial education article, not a broad AI glossary.

Strique's brand DNA gives the angle: an agentic AI marketing platform should plan work, ship work, measure outcomes, and feed learnings into the next run. The article should help buyers sort workflow depth from thin AI wrappers.

## Search Intent

Searchers are likely comparing platform categories, product capabilities, and evaluation criteria. They need a clear framework for deciding what belongs in an AI marketing platform and what still needs human review.

## Content Structure

1. Open with the buying mistake: treating the category like a smarter dashboard.
2. Define the operating loop an AI marketing platform should support.
3. Lay out evaluation criteria for context, execution, connectors, analytics, approvals, memory, reporting, and ownership.
4. Call out weak buying signals.
5. Explain how Strique fits without claiming objective superiority.
6. Give a practical checklist for buyer conversations.
