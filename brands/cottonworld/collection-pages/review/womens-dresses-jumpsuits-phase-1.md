## Build an outfit around what you own

A dress or jumpsuit can be the main piece, with the rest of the outfit shaped by the weather, your plans and the clothes already in your wardrobe. Keep the combination familiar if the day is busy. A layer can be useful for a commute or an indoor setting, while comfortable footwear can help when your plans involve walking. Adapt these ideas to your own dress code and preferences.

- **For a workday:** Check that the neckline, hem and sleeves suit your workplace. Add a layer you can remove if your day moves between indoor and outdoor settings.
- **For a relaxed lunch:** Choose a fit and length that feel comfortable while sitting and walking. Keep accessories simple if you want the garment to remain the focus.
- **For travel:** Look at the closure and care directions before packing. Pair the piece with footwear you already trust for walking, and consider whether the style works with the layers you plan to bring.
- **For repeat wear:** Bring the same dress back with a different layer or shoes. A jumpsuit can also be worn with familiar accessories, provided they suit the setting and the garment's neckline.
- **For changing plans:** Choose around comfort and coverage first. A removable layer can make an outfit easier to adapt as the day unfolds.

These are starting points, not rules. Wear the piece in a way that feels natural to you and fits the places you go.
