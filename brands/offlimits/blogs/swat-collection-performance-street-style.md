## H2: How to Style S.W.A.T. from Workout to Weekend

The shoe already supplies visual weight. The rest of the outfit only needs to balance it.

### H3: Balance the Proportions

Let tapered joggers finish near the collar, or allow straight and relaxed trousers to fall over part of the upper. Very narrow bottoms exaggerate a chunky shoe, which can work when deliberate but feels less effortless for everyday styling.

### H3: Repeat One Colour

With the Olive/Black Sports Edition, repeat black in a tee or jacket. With the Cream/Mocha Street Edition, echo cream in a sweatshirt or cap and let the mocha panels stand on their own. Matching every shade turns a confident outfit into a uniform.

### H3: Change One Layer After the Session

Keep the same tee and joggers, then add an overshirt, bomber or utility jacket. The substantial S.W.A.T. shape can carry that extra layer, so the outfit shifts from training-led to street-ready without a full change.
