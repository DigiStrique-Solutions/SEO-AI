How to Care for Your Summer Clothes

[P00052 | 5036:5037 | NORMAL_TEXT]
⟦EMPTY PARAGRAPH⟧

[P00053 | 5037:5154 | NORMAL_TEXT]
Sweat, sunscreen, and saltwater can be tough on clothes. Here is how to keep your summer wardrobe looking brand new.

[P00054 | 5154:5155 | NORMAL_TEXT]
⟦EMPTY PARAGRAPH⟧

[P00055 | 5155:5302 | NORMAL_TEXT]
1. Wash on Cold: Hot water will shrink your linen and cotton pieces. Always stick to cold washes to preserve the fit and the bright summer colors.

[P00056 | 5302:5455 | NORMAL_TEXT]
2. Wash After Heavy Sweating: Don't let sweat and sunscreen sit in the fabric for days. Wash your pieces promptly to avoid yellowing around the collars.

[P00057 | 5455:5627 | NORMAL_TEXT]
3. Absolutely No Dryers: The tumble dryer will bake the life out of natural fibers. Hang your clothes up outside in the shade and let the summer breeze dry them naturally.

[P00058 | 5627:5781 | NORMAL_TEXT]
4. Don't Over-Iron Linen: Embrace the wrinkles! Linen is supposed to look relaxed and slightly textured. A quick run over with a steamer is all you need.

3. Is it okay to wear dark colors in the summer?

[P00082 | 6700:6946 | NORMAL_TEXT]
You certainly can, especially for evening events. However, during the peak daytime sun, dark colors (like black and dark navy) absorb heat and will make you feel noticeably hotter. Try to stick to whites, pastels, and light greys during the day.
