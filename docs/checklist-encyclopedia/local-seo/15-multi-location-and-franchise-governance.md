## Item 1

Item ID: `local-seo.15-multi-location-and-franchise-governance.216ac0d1`

Original checklist item: There is a single source of truth for locations, NAP, hours, categories, services, owners, and landing page URLs.

### What It Means

Confirm whether the audited scope satisfies this requirement: "There is a single source of truth for locations, NAP, hours, categories, services, owners, and landing page URLs." In practice, this means the reviewer needs concrete evidence, not a general impression.

### Why It Affects SEO

Local SEO depends on accurate entity, location, category, review, and profile data. Inconsistency can weaken relevance, prominence, and customer trust.

### How To Verify

1. Identify the exact URL, template, brand workspace, connector account, or page group in scope.
2. Collect evidence from: GBP, CMS/code, GSC, GA4, Firecrawl, Human/context, CRM/calls, Google Maps.
3. Record pass, fail, not_applicable, or not_checked_blocked in the audit matrix with an artifact reference.
