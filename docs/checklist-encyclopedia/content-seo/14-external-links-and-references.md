## Item 1

Item ID: `content-seo.14-external-links-and-references.d47bba42`

Original checklist item: External links support claims, definitions, standards, research, or tools.

### What It Means

Confirm whether the audited scope satisfies this requirement: "External links support claims, definitions, standards, research, or tools." In practice, this means the reviewer needs concrete evidence, not a general impression.

### Why It Affects SEO

Useful, source-backed content improves relevance, user confidence, and eligibility for sensitive or competitive queries. Weak or unsupported content can suppress trust and conversions.
