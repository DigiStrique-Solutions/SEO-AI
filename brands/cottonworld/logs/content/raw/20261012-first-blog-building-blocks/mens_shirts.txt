We do not cut corners when it comes to the materials resting against your skin. Natural comfort is the core of our brand, and here is why it matters.
1. 100% Breathable Cotton
Forget synthetic blends that make you sweat before you even leave the house. Pure cotton breathes naturally, allowing air to circulate around your torso. It absorbs moisture and softens up beautifully with every single wash, meaning your shirt actually gets better over time.

How to Make Your Cotton & Linen Shirts Last
Premium shirts deserve proper care. Follow these steps, and they will look sharp for years.
1. Wash on Cold:
Heat is the enemy of natural fibers. Wash your shirts in cold water to completely avoid shrinkage and protect the vibrant colors.
2. Button Them Up:
Before throwing them in the machine, button the shirt up and turn it inside out. This protects the buttons from chipping and the collar from losing its shape.
3. Ditch the Dryer:
Never tumble dry your cotton and linen shirts. Hang them up on a good hanger and let them air dry in the shade.
4. Ironing:
Iron cotton shirts while they are still just a tiny bit damp. For linen, use a high heat setting with plenty of steam, or just embrace the natural, relaxed wrinkles!

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
1. Can I wear a casual cotton shirt to a formal event?
It depends on the shirt and the event. A crisp, perfectly ironed, solid-colored cotton shirt (like white or light blue) tucked into tailored chinos works beautifully for smart-casual and semi-formal events. However, leave the bold prints and half-sleeves for the weekend.
2. Should I tuck my shirt in or leave it out?
As a general rule, if the hem of the shirt is flat, it is meant to be worn untucked. If the hem is curved (longer in the front and back, shorter on the sides), it is traditionally designed to be tucked in. However, our casual shirts feature a perfectly balanced hem length so you can comfortably wear them both ways.
3. Does 100% linen wrinkle easily?
Yes, pure linen will naturally crease as you wear it. That is a hallmark of the premium fabric, not a flaw! It provides a relaxed, sophisticated charm. If you want a crisper look for the office, a quick run over with a steam iron works perfectly.
4. How should a men’s shirt fit?
The shoulder seams should sit exactly at the edge of your shoulders. You should be able to fit two fingers comfortably between your neck and the buttoned collar. The sleeves should end right where your thumb meets your wrist, and the body should lightly drape your torso without billowing out at the waist.
5. Are your shirts pre-shrunk?
We use high-quality, pre-washed natural fabrics to minimize shrinkage. However, pure cotton and linen can still experience very minor tightening after the first wash. Sticking to cold water and air drying ensures your shirt retains its perfect, original fit.
