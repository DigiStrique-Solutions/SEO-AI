## Item 1

Item ID: `local-seo.20-common-anti-patterns.cc3faa67`

Original checklist item: Keyword-stuffed business names.

### What It Means

Confirm whether the audited scope satisfies this requirement: "Keyword-stuffed business names." In practice, this means the reviewer needs concrete evidence, not a general impression.

### Why It Affects SEO

Local SEO depends on accurate entity, location, category, review, and profile data. Inconsistency can weaken relevance, prominence, and customer trust. Intent alignment affects whether the page deserves to show for the target query. This check keeps the page focused on the searcher's job instead of only matching keywords.

### How To Verify

1. Identify the exact URL, template, brand workspace, connector account, or page group in scope.

3. Record pass, fail, not_applicable, or not_checked_blocked in the audit matrix with an artifact reference.

### Evidence Sources
