## Summary

The guide compares the cut, comfort, and styling effect of straight-fit and slim-fit jeans. It helps readers choose based on desired silhouette, body shape, and personal comfort rather than treating one fit as universally better.

## Key points

- Straight-fit jeans provide a roomier, more relaxed line.
- Slim-fit jeans create a closer, tailored silhouette.
- The right choice depends on comfort, styling preference, and body shape.
