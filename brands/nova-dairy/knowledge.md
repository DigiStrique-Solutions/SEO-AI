# Nova Dairy — working brand knowledge

## Identity and positioning

Nova Dairy is the consumer-facing dairy brand of Sterling Agro Industries. The site traces the business to 1991 and positions the brand around pure, safe, tasty dairy for Indian homes. The company also recruits distributors, takes bulk enquiries, supplies dairy ingredients to food and FMCG businesses, and exports internationally.

## Messaging to preserve

- Lead with purity, freshness, familiar taste and family trust.
- Connect product claims to observable process evidence: farmer sourcing, quality checks, filtration, pasteurisation, packaging, cold-chain and manufacturing capacity.
- Use approachable Indian food language: home, family, chai, sabzi, paratha and familiar recipes.
- Keep the tone warm and direct; explain technical processes in ordinary language.
- For distributors, focus on product demand, earnings potential and customer satisfaction without inventing financial outcomes.
- For B2B buyers, emphasize manufacturing capability, quality controls, dairy ingredient applications and export experience only where sourced.

## Messaging constraints

- Do not invent health, nutrition, purity, safety, certification, market-share or environmental claims.
- Treat numerical claims as site-reported until supporting documents are supplied.
- Do not call Nova Dairy India's largest, best or most trusted brand without independent criteria and evidence.
- Do not imply that every product is available in every Indian market or export country.
- Do not publish comparative pricing or superiority claims against Amul, Mother Dairy or other brands without current evidence.

## Audiences and conversions

Primary audiences are Indian household dairy buyers and distribution partners. Secondary audiences include retailers, bulk buyers, food manufacturers, institutional procurement teams, international importers and prospective employees.

The website supports four conversion paths: general enquiry, bulk-order enquiry, distributor application and careers. Product pages also expose buying intent for selected items.

## Products and content themes

Observed categories include shudh ghee, cow ghee, paneer, butter, dairy whitener, skimmed milk powder, buttermilk, dahi, dairy creamer, flavoured milk and milk. The Nutrition Centre publishes family recipes using dairy products and is the site's main editorial/content hub.

## Operational proof surfaced on the site

The site reports 20+ quality checks, three manufacturing plants, more than 26 lakh litres of milk handled daily, exports to 33+ countries, and farmer sourcing in Madhya Pradesh, Haryana and Uttar Pradesh. It also names processing approaches such as filtration, pasteurisation, nozzle drying and Bactofuge. These are first-party claims and require substantiation before use in regulated, comparative or high-stakes contexts.
