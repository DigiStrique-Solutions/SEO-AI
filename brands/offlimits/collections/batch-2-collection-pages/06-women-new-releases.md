## Give a new pair a role in the wardrobe you already have

Fresh footwear does not have to mean rebuilding every outfit around it. Start with clothes you already reach for. A clean sneaker can sit easily with a tee and denim. A running-inspired pair can work with leggings and a training layer. A more expressive colour can become the detail that changes a familiar look, while a neutral pair may move easily through more of the week.

Look at the upper, closure, sole and insock information for the styles that make your shortlist. Those details describe the product more clearly than a photograph can. Lace-ups can help you adjust the fit; the stated sole and insock materials tell you what the brand lists for the underfoot construction. Use that information alongside the activity you have planned, the socks you normally wear and the amount of room you like around the toes.

Fit deserves a place early in the process. Check the live size options before you settle on a colour, and use the size you normally choose as a starting point. If a pair is meant for an active session, consider whether you want the heel to feel held in place. If it is for everyday plans, think about the distance you expect to cover and the outfits it needs to work with. When it arrives, an easy first outing around home or on a familiar route can help you decide how it belongs in your rotation.
