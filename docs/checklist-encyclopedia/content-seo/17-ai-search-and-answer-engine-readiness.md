# 17. AI Search And Answer Engine Readiness

## Item 1

Item ID: `content-seo.17-ai-search-and-answer-engine-readiness.763aaefb`

Original checklist item: The page is eligible for normal indexing before AI visibility is considered.

### What It Means

Confirm whether the audited scope satisfies this requirement: "The page is eligible for normal indexing before AI visibility is considered." In practice, this means the reviewer needs concrete evidence, not a general impression.

### Why It Affects SEO

Useful, source-backed content improves relevance, user confidence, and eligibility for sensitive or competitive queries. Weak or unsupported content can suppress trust and conversions. Answer engines need crawlable, clear, well-supported information they can cite or summarize. This check improves extractability, factual grounding, and mention quality.

### How To Verify

1. Identify the exact URL, template, brand workspace, connector account, or page group in scope.
2. Collect evidence from: GSC, GA4, Manual/free SERP, Firecrawl, Playwright, CMS/code, Human/context, saved prompt set, manual AI SERP.
3. Record prompt wording, platform, location, date, account state, cited URLs, and answer summary.
4. Record pass, fail, not_applicable, or not_checked_blocked in the audit matrix with an artifact reference.

### Evidence Sources

GSC, GA4, Manual/free SERP, Firecrawl, Playwright, CMS/code, Human/context, saved prompt set, manual AI SERP

### Pass Criteria

The item is verified as true for the scoped URL, template, brand, connector account, or page group, and the audit row includes a specific evidence source and artifact reference.
