## Item 1

Item ID: `local-seo.12-localbusiness-structured-data-and-entity-clarity.aa6f5d56`

Original checklist item: LocalBusiness or a more specific subtype is used where it accurately matches the visible business.

### What It Means

Confirm whether the audited scope satisfies this requirement: "LocalBusiness or a more specific subtype is used where it accurately matches the visible business." In practice, this means the reviewer needs concrete evidence, not a general impression.

Local SEO depends on accurate entity, location, category, review, and profile data. Inconsistency can weaken relevance, prominence, and customer trust.
