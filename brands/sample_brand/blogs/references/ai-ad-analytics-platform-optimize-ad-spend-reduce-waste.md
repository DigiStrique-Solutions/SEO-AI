# Reference: AI analytics platforms that track ad waste and optimize ad performance

**Summary:** Positions Strique as a solution to ad waste through chat-based execution rather than dashboard analysis, arguing that faster action — not more reporting — drives AI ad performance. Shows how AI platforms identify inefficiencies and enable rapid optimization across Meta and Google simultaneously.

**Key points:**
- Common sources of ad waste: low-intention audiences, weak geo targeting, poor placements, creative fatigue, tracking discrepancies.
- Platform must-haves: unified Meta/Google visibility, automated waste detection, actionable recommendations, A/B testing guidance.
- Chat-based execution collapses insight-to-action from hours to minutes.
- Seven pain points: slow reporting, unclear waste sources, budget misallocation, creative exhaustion, unsystematic testing, launch overhead, reactive management.
- Differentiator: outcome-driven prompts + integrated execution vs. reporting-only tools.
