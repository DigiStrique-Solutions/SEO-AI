Scope: the supplied men’s landing page, all 24 men-specific collection destinations found in its body/navigation, the shared Top Picks destination, and 10 additional collection URLs from the reference sheet (35 collection URLs in total). Full available collection descriptions, headings and related-collection anchors were reviewed. Product-detail pages and paginated product inventories are outside this content review.
Evidence: live HTTP responses and extracted page content, the supplied sheet, and browser checks of T-shirts, Shirts, Joggers, Kurtas and New Arrivals. All recommended replacement collection URLs returned HTTP 200 with the expected collection identity.
