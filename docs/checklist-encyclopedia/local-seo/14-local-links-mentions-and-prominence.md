## Item 1

Item ID: `local-seo.14-local-links-mentions-and-prominence.5b77ba6d`

Original checklist item: Local backlinks and mentions are reviewed for relevance, trust, and business value.

### What It Means

Confirm whether the audited scope satisfies this requirement: "Local backlinks and mentions are reviewed for relevance, trust, and business value." In practice, this means the reviewer needs concrete evidence, not a general impression.

### Why It Affects SEO

Useful, source-backed content improves relevance, user confidence, and eligibility for sensitive or competitive queries. Weak or unsupported content can suppress trust and conversions. Local SEO depends on accurate entity, location, category, review, and profile data. Inconsistency can weaken relevance, prominence, and customer trust.
