Original checklist item: Listings are prioritized by audience relevance, trust, and referral value.

### What It Means

Confirm whether the audited scope satisfies this requirement: "Listings are prioritized by audience relevance, trust, and referral value." In practice, this means the reviewer needs concrete evidence, not a general impression.
