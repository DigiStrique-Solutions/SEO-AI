## Summary

This recipe describes carrots slow-cooked with Nova Pasteurised Milk and Shudh Ghee, then enriched with skimmed milk powder, sugar, cardamom, nuts and saffron. It presents gajar ka halwa as a warm winter or celebration dessert.

- Slow-cooks the carrots in Nova Pasteurised Milk.
