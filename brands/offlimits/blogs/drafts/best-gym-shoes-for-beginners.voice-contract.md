## Swap test

Could a competitor publish this after find-and-replacing the brand name? No — the spine argues that a beginner
should *not* be sold up, names the entry model by name, and routes size 12–14 readers to Big & Tall. That
argument is only available to a brand whose whole position is accessible performance with extended sizing.

## Note on the open question

`brand-dna.json` `open_questions` records a reviewer asking for an "aspirational" voice, which conflicts with a
DNA that positions *against* aspiration. Per CLAUDE.md the brand file wins. This draft reads that feedback as
"the copy was flat" and answers it with the brand's own energy (gutsy, direct, Tribe), not with luxury framing.
