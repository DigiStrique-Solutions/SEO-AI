## Item 1

Item ID: `site-architecture-seo.22-international-and-multilingual-architecture.3926fcd1`

Original checklist item: The international structure is explicit: ccTLD, subdomain, subdirectory, or parameter-based.

### What It Means

Confirm whether the audited scope satisfies this requirement: "The international structure is explicit: ccTLD, subdomain, subdirectory, or parameter-based." In practice, this means the reviewer needs concrete evidence, not a general impression.

### Why It Affects SEO

Architecture controls how users and crawlers find important pages. Good internal linking helps distribute context, priority, and crawl paths.
