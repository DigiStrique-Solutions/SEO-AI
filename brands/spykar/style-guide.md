## Purpose and authority

Use this guide for all Spykar blog drafting, editing and review. It is based on Spykar's existing published tone and content style. For blog work, these rules override generic content defaults. FAQs may be integrated into the article flow or placed at the end; they are not standalone resources.

## Brand voice

The Spykar voice is:

- **Youthful and expressive:** reflects individuality, confidence and modern identity.
- **Trend-aware:** current and fashion-focused without becoming overly technical.
- **Relatable:** speaks directly to everyday lifestyles and real situations.
- **Effortlessly stylish:** prioritises comfort, ease and wearable fashion.

The tone is:

- Conversational without becoming slang-heavy.
- Confident without exaggeration.
- Aspirational but grounded in everyday wearability.
- Energetic and modern, reflecting youth culture.
- Descriptive while remaining easy to understand.

## Blog structure

### Title

- Use Sentence case or Title Case.
- Keep it clear, trend-focused and engaging.
- Include the primary keyword.

Example: `Summer denim trends 2026: What's in for men and women`

### Introduction

Write one or two paragraphs, usually 80–120 words:

- Start with a strong hook or seasonal reference.
- Introduce the topic or trend.
- Use the primary keyword naturally.
- Establish a fresh, modern and context-appropriate tone.

### Body

Use five to eight thematic sections:

- Structure content with H2 and H3 headings.
- Use one or two short paragraphs per subsection.
- Explain trends, styles or ideas clearly.
- Add relevant styling suggestions.
- Cover useful trend breakdowns, outfit inspiration, fabric and fit insights, and seasonal styling.

### Styling section

Include a dedicated styling section in most blogs:

- Provide practical outfit ideas.
- Give realistic, day-to-day styling tips.
- Add occasion-based suggestions where useful.
- Keep the advice easy to follow.

### Brand integration

- Integrate Spykar subtly and naturally.
- Avoid aggressive selling.
- Position Spykar as a style enabler, not merely a seller.

Example: `Spykar's latest collection brings these trends to life…`

### Conclusion and CTA

Write one paragraph, usually 80–120 words:

- Summarise the main ideas.
- Reinforce style, comfort and versatility.
- Close with a soft CTA.

CTA patterns include:

- `Explore the latest styles…`
- `Upgrade your wardrobe…`
- `Discover your summer look…`

### FAQs

FAQs are optional but recommended. When included:

- Use five or six relevant questions where the topic supports them.
- Give one-line or short answers.
- Keep answers clear, direct, useful and conversational.
- Focus on styling, trends or usability.
- Avoid technical jargon.

## Keyword rules

### Formatting

- Do not bold keywords.
- Use keywords naturally within sentences.
- Never sacrifice readability for placement.

### Placement

Use the primary keyword in:

- The title.
- The introduction.
- At least one heading.

Distribute secondary keywords across relevant sections, especially styling and trend discussions.

### Frequency

- Use keywords organically.
- Avoid repetition and keyword stuffing.
- Use natural variations where useful.

## Grammar and formatting

- Use Indian English: `colour`, `favourite`, and equivalent spellings.
- Prefer present tense.
- Contractions such as `it's` and `you're` are allowed.
- Titles may use Title Case or Sentence case.
- Prefer sentence case for headings.
- Avoid unnecessary capitalisation.
- Keep paragraphs short, usually two to four lines.
- Add spacing between sections.
- Use bullet points sparingly.
- Avoid dense text blocks.

## Digital-first writing

Write for a mobile-friendly, scannable reading experience:

- Use clear section breaks and descriptive headings.
- Describe outfits visually.
- Mention colours, fits and textures clearly.
- Avoid specific product names unless the brief requires them.

## Fashion-writing approach

Prioritise:

- Comfort and style together.
- Seasonal relevance.
- Versatility.
- Real-life usability.

Replace vague statements with concrete descriptions.

Avoid: `This outfit looks stylish.`

Prefer: `This look pairs relaxed denim with breathable layers, making it perfect for all-day summer wear.`

## Content themes

Spykar blogs typically cover:

- Seasonal trends, including summer, winter and festive dressing.
- Denim-focused fashion.
- Outfit guides.
- Styling tips.
- Trend breakdowns.

Every blog should feel relatable, reflect modern youth lifestyles and encourage self-expression.

Core thought: **Fashion should feel effortless, personal and expressive.**

## Meta content

### Meta title

- Target 50–60 characters.
- Include the primary keyword.
- Keep it clear and engaging.

### Meta description

- Target 140–160 characters.
- Include keywords naturally.
- Write to encourage relevant clicks.

## Workflow and review

### Drafting

- Follow the defined structure.
- Maintain the brand tone and readability.

### SEO check

- Confirm natural keyword placement.
- Remove stuffing and unnecessary repetition.

### Content review

- Check flow, clarity and tone.
- Confirm consistency with the Spykar voice.

### Final edits

- Refine the introduction and conclusion.
- Improve readability and engagement.

### Submission

- Submit clean, formatted, ready-to-publish content.
