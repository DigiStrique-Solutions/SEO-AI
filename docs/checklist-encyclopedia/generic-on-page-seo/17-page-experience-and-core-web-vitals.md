## Item 1

Item ID: `generic-on-page-seo.17-page-experience-and-core-web-vitals.9b085e60`

Original checklist item: LCP is under 2.5 seconds for good field performance.

### What It Means

Confirm whether the audited scope satisfies this requirement: "LCP is under 2.5 seconds for good field performance." In practice, this means the reviewer needs concrete evidence, not a general impression.

### Why It Affects SEO

Intent alignment affects whether the page deserves to show for the target query. This check keeps the page focused on the searcher's job instead of only matching keywords. Mobile rendering, accessibility, and performance influence user success and can affect search quality signals, crawl efficiency, and conversion quality.

### How To Verify

1. Identify the exact URL, template, brand workspace, connector account, or page group in scope.
2. Collect evidence from: CrUX, LH, Playwright, Firecrawl, GSC, GA4, OSS, Lighthouse, PageSpeed.
3. Record pass, fail, not_applicable, or not_checked_blocked in the audit matrix with an artifact reference.

### Evidence Sources

CrUX, LH, Playwright, Firecrawl, GSC, GA4, OSS, Lighthouse, PageSpeed
