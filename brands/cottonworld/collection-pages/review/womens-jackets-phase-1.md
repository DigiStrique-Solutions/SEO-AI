## A simple styling guide

Begin with what the day asks of you. A jacket can be an extra layer for a commute, an afternoon out or a plan that carries into the evening. Pair it with clothes that already feel familiar, and let the details do only as much as you want them to.

- **For an everyday outing:** Wear a jacket over a simple top with women’s pants. Choose a fit that feels comfortable as you move through errands or a walk.
- **For a workday:** A solid colour can sit easily alongside pieces you already wear to work. Check the collar and opening to decide whether you prefer it buttoned or open.
- **For a relaxed plan:** A boxy fit gives a different outline from a regular fit. Keep the rest of the combination simple if you want the jacket to be the main layer.
- **For travel:** Consider how the jacket works with more than one outfit in your bag. A familiar colour can make it easier to repeat the layer across different plans.
- **For layering:** Check sleeve and shoulder details against the piece you plan to wear underneath. Comfort when moving matters more than a label alone.
- **For repeat wear:** Bring the same jacket into different combinations by changing the top, bottom or footwear. Repeating a useful piece can keep everyday choices straightforward.

These are ideas to adapt, not rules to follow. The aim is to choose a layer that makes sense for the day and leaves your attention for everything else happening in it.
