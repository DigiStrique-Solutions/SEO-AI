## A simple styling guide

A blouse can move between different parts of a week when you choose it with the rest of your routine in mind. Start with where you are going, what you will be doing and what is already in your wardrobe.

- **For a workday:** Try a collared blouse with tailored trousers. A regular-fit silhouette can be an easy place to start; choose a colour that feels appropriate for your workplace.
- **For brunch or a relaxed plan:** A printed blouse or a brighter colour can be the one detail that gives a simple outfit its character. Keep the other pieces familiar.
- **For travel:** Choose a blouse you can pair with more than one bottom. A colour you already reach for can make outfit planning more straightforward.
- **For layering:** Compare sleeve and neckline details with the layer you plan to wear over it. A more open collar or a shorter sleeve can change how the layers sit.
- **For repeat wear:** Repeating a favourite is practical. Rotate the bottoms, shoes or layer around it instead of needing a new outfit for each plan.

These are outfit ideas rather than rules. The point is to make a choice that fits the day you actually have, whether it includes a commute, time with friends or a quieter few hours at home.
