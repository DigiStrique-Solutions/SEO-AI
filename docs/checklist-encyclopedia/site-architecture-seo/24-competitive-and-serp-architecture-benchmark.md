## Item 1

Item ID: `site-architecture-seo.24-competitive-and-serp-architecture-benchmark.2d1c94b9`

Original checklist item: Top organic competitors are identified by query set, not only by known business competitors.

### What It Means

Confirm whether the audited scope satisfies this requirement: "Top organic competitors are identified by query set, not only by known business competitors." In practice, this means the reviewer needs concrete evidence, not a general impression.

### Why It Affects SEO

Architecture controls how users and crawlers find important pages. Good internal linking helps distribute context, priority, and crawl paths. Intent alignment affects whether the page deserves to show for the target query. This check keeps the page focused on the searcher's job instead of only matching keywords.

### How To Verify

1. Identify the exact URL, template, brand workspace, connector account, or page group in scope.
2. Collect evidence from: Manual/free SERP, GKP, GSC, Firecrawl, Human/context.
3. Use Search Console data when access exists; otherwise mark the row as blocked with the missing property or permission.
4. Record pass, fail, not_applicable, or not_checked_blocked in the audit matrix with an artifact reference.

### Evidence Sources

Manual/free SERP, GKP, GSC, Firecrawl, Human/context
