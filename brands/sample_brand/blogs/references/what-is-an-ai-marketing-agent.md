# Reference: What is an AI marketing agent? The 2026 complete guide

**Summary:** Frames the AI marketing agent as an architectural shift from passive tools to autonomous systems that run the full workflow — research, planning, content, launch, measurement, iteration. The key differentiator is persistent memory that compounds business knowledge over time.

**Key points:**
- AI marketing agents are autonomous — they execute full workflows without human intervention at every step.
- A memory layer accumulates business context over time, improving output through learning rather than model updates.
- Production-grade agents research, plan, create, launch, measure, and iterate across channels automatically.
- Evaluation criteria: real system integration, performance read/adjust, persistent memory, approval gates, goal decomposition.
- Reported 30–50% ROAS improvement within the first quarter after adoption.
