## FAQs

### What should I wear on a humid rainy day?

Choose a cotton T-shirt with trousers, a light shirt over a tee, or a simple dress. Keep your bag hands-free and choose footwear with reliable grip.

### Is cotton good for rainy season outfits?

Cotton is a comfortable everyday option for humid days. For rainy season outfits, also consider your commute, a manageable hem and carrying a dry spare layer.

### What are easy monsoon outfit ideas for work?

Try a regular-fit shirt with dark pants, or a blouse with trousers. Keep accessories light and use an easy-to-carry umbrella.

### How can I make monsoon fashion feel polished?

Choose two or three coordinated colours, a simple silhouette and functional accessories. A comfortable outfit looks more polished.
