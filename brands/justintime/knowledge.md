# Just In Time — working knowledge

## Positioning observed
The site presents Just In Time Trading Private Limited as a multi-brand watch retailer in India, with ecommerce, stores and watch-service routes. These are first-party claims, not independent verification.

## Audience and paths
The storefront supports shopping by gender, brand, style, price, movement and gifting. Store-locator and service journeys are visible. Purchase and store/service actions are inferred goals.

## Voice and content
Public copy is premium/aspirational; product pages provide model-level descriptions and specifications. Collection pages include editorial headings and FAQs.

## Measurement and evidence
- GSC: no justintime.in property was present in the connected verified-property list on 2026-09-29.
- Analytics: platform/property not confirmed; do not assume GA4 or PostHog.
- PageSpeed/CrUX: mobile homepage sample only; lab and field metrics are separate.
- Public crawl/render: five content URLs sampled; the multi-page crawl timed out.

## Factual conflict
Homepage metadata and llms.txt say “75+ stores”; About Us says “100+ stores.” Verify the approved current claim before reuse.

## Audit notes
The 2026-09-29 audit is partial. It found long sampled title/description tags, an empty homepage H1, repeated H1s on one collection, mismatched ItemList/HowTo schema and weak mobile homepage performance. See audits/summary.md and audit JSON for evidence.
