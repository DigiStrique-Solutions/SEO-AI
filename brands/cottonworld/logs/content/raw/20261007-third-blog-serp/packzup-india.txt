Climate & weather India has three seasons. Winter (October-March) is cool and dry — best for travel. Summer (April-June) is extreme — 40°C+ in north. Monsoon (July-September) brings heavy rains, especially on west coast. Mountains stay cool year-round. Must-have items for India Modest clothing Cover shoulders, knees, and chest. Light cotton kurta, harem pants, or long skirts work everywhere. Bottled water filter (LifeStraw/SteriPen) Tap water unsafe. Use filtered or sealed bottled water only. Hand sanitizer + wet wipes Many bathrooms lack soap and toilet paper. Carry both. Imodium + electrolyte tablets "Delhi belly" affects ~50% of travelers. Be prepared. Mosquito repellent (30%+ DEET) Dengue + malaria risk. Apply at dusk/dawn especially. Lightweight scarf/sarong For temples (head cover), sun protection, picnic blanket. Sturdy sandals + closed shoes Sandals for general wear, closed shoes for rural/temple visits. Cash (small bills) Many places cash-only. Withdraw at SBI/HDFC ATMs for best rates. SIM card photo copies Need passport copy + photo for India SIM. Get pre-cut for Airtel/Jio. Indian outlet adapter (Type D/M) Indian-style 3-prong plug. Universal adapter must have Type D. Travel tips Avoid eating raw vegetables and salads (water-washed). Stick to hot, freshly-cooked food. Negotiate rickshaw fares before getting in. Use Uber/Ola apps for transparent pricing. Cover head/shoulders when entering temples and gurdwaras. Public displays of affection are frowned upon in most regions.
