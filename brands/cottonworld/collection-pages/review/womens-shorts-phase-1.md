## A practical styling guide

Start with the plan, not a styling rule. Shorts can be one option for a warm day, a relaxed weekend or a trip where you want familiar pieces that are easy to combine. Build around the activities you have and the clothes you already like wearing.

- **For a day out:** Pair a solid pair of shorts with a top or T-shirt you reach for often. Choose a waist and length that feel comfortable for walking and sitting.
- **For brunch:** A printed or coloured short can bring a little character to a simple outfit. Keep the other pieces familiar if you want the look to feel easy.
- **For travel:** Think about the tops and shoes you are packing. A pair that works with more than one combination can make outfit planning simpler.
- **For time at home:** Start with the waist detail and fabric you prefer. Choose a pair that feels at ease with the rest of your day.
- **For a changeable plan:** Keep a light layer nearby if your day moves between different settings. Choose the shorts around how you expect to spend most of your time.
- **For repeat wear:** Bring a useful pair back into rotation and vary the top or layer when you want a different combination. Repeating something you enjoy wearing is a practical choice.

These ideas are starting points, not rules. Comfort, coverage and your own routine are better guides than dressing for an occasion in a particular way.
